A post-wedding trip

Exploring North India

← Back to the wedding home

After the wedding, a few of us travel on through Rajasthan and Agra together. Here is the plan in two versions, built for a big group, so think minibus rather than cars.

Nothing is booked yet. Hotels, prices and flight times are marked to confirm, and drive times are rough estimates.

The Fixed Points

Thu 25 FebTravel starts. Wed 24 Feb is a rest and check-out day.
Thu 4 MarEveryone back in Jaipur by the evening at the latest.
Fri 5 MarJaipur to Delhi, with a night at an airport hotel (Aerocity or Mahipalpur). Pickup around 01:30.
Sat 6 MarInternational flight from Delhi at 05:00.

The Two Routes

Two versions of the same loop. Route 1 flies in to Jaisalmer for the desert and forts; Route 2 skips the flight and adds Ranakpur and Kumbhalgarh by road. From Mon 1 March onwards they are identical.

A note on scope: we have kept this to Rajasthan and Agra. February is lovely in the north but not the season for South India, and Rajasthan is the state for forts, palaces and desert, so one region gives you the best of it without long detours. Delhi sightseeing, Pushkar, Ranthambore and the hill towns are left out to keep the driving sane for a big group.

Book These First

  • Taj Mahal tickets (foreign visitors): book online ahead at tajmahal.gov.in. Prices to confirm. Closed on Fridays.
  • Udaipur lake-view hotels on Lake Pichola: book early, February is peak season. Hotels and prices to confirm (aiming for about $100 a night at most).
  • Jodhpur fort-side hotels with Mehrangarh views: book early. Hotels and prices to confirm.
  • Jaisalmer flights (Route 1 only): confirm the IndiGo Jaipur to Jaisalmer route runs on 25 Feb and that there are seats for the whole group.
  • Minibus or tempo traveller with driver for the Rajasthan leg: Route 1 from Jaisalmer on 26 Feb to Jaipur on 3 Mar; Route 2 from Jaipur on 25 Feb to Jaipur on 3 Mar.
  • Delhi transfer and airport hotel for 5 to 6 Mar: separate transport for the Jaipur to Delhi leg, a hotel in Aerocity or Mahipalpur, and a 01:30 pickup. Our planner’s team may have vetted drivers.

Good to Know

  • No Jaisalmer flight? If it doesn’t run or seats are short, take Route 2.
  • Taj closed Fridays. Agra sits on Tue 2 and Wed 3 March here, so no clash.
  • Jaipur pitstop (Mon 1 Mar): splits the long Udaipur–Agra run into two easier drives.
  • Shorter option: if everyone can meet in Delhi, end in Agra and drive Agra–Delhi on 5 Mar (~4h15), saving ~6h.
  • Festivals: Maha Shivaratri Sat 6 Mar (some calendars 5 Mar), temples busy. Holi is 22 Mar, after the trip.
  • Food and stops: keep stops few for a big group; roadside food is limited, so eat in the cities.
