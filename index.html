Getting There

By Air

From Paris, we’d recommend Emirates via Dubai to Jaipur (JAI), around 13 hours in total including the layover. Air France from CDG to Jaipur is another option, usually with a layover (often via Amsterdam and Delhi), roughly 15–20 hours all in. You can also fly Paris to Delhi direct and take a taxi the rest of the way: about 300 km, roughly a 4–5 hour drive.

By Train

Jaipur Junction is well connected to Delhi and other major cities. Pre-booked taxis and ride apps are available at the station.

Local Transport

Good to Know

Do I need a visa for India?

Most international guests can apply for an e-Visa online. Check requirements for your nationality well in advance.

What will the weather be like?

February in Jaipur is pleasant — warm, sunny days (around 25°C) and cooler evenings. Bring a light layer for the night events.

What currency should I bring?

The Indian Rupee (INR). Cards are widely accepted in hotels; carry some cash for smaller purchases and tips.

What food will be served?

It’s a fully vegan wedding — all food and drink is plant-based, with plenty of options throughout. If you have any specific dietary requirements, please contact us in advance so we can make arrangements.
